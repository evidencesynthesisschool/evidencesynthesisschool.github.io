Registration fee

Registration for the International School costs 500 Euro. This fee covers the cost of all training, including certification and all programme activities, along with refreshments and some food and drink. The following are included in the fee:


  • All teaching activities
  • Mid-morning/afternoon refreshments (tea/coffee/fresh drinks and biscuits)
  • School dinner (drinks and delicious Andalusian tapas)
  • Excursion to a local nature reserve including transport and entrance

The fee does NOT include travel to Almería, hotel accommodation, or travel from your hotel to the School each day.



Registration has been kept as low as possible to make the course affordable. Participants from Low- and Middle- Income Countries, Spain and Portugal can apply for financial support with their tuition directly from the University of Almería. This process is handled by the University and whilst we can advise you on the process, we unfortunately are not included in any decisions. According to the University of Almería's policies, European and non-European students can apply for financial support (the policy can be read here in Spanish: https://www.ual.es/estudios/gestionesacademicas/becas/ayudasestudio). If you would like to eqnuire about applying for a bursary, please contact María López Rodríguez from UAL for more information.




Registration process

Registration is not yet open - check back in mid July to register! When registration opens, you will have until the end of September to secure your place. You can cancel any time before this date and we will arrange a full refund (manually). Cancellations after this date cannot be refunded, unless there are evidenced extenuating circumstances.
